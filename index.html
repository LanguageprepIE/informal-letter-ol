👨‍🏫 About this site: This website was created by David Martín, a native Spanish teacher with many years of experience teaching post-primary students in Ireland.
It was designed to support Ordinary Level students and learners who may find languages more challenging.
There are many resources available for Higher Level, but fewer adapted to OL learners.
The goal of this site is to offer authentic, clear and accessible materials so that every student —whatever their goals— can make progress and feel confident learning Spanish.
