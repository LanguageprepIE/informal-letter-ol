Listening Comprehension

Leaving Certificate · Ordinary Level

Listen, understand and practise at your own pace. / Escucha, comprende y practica a tu ritmo.

How it will work / Cómo funcionará

Each listening will have an actual audio recording, four or five questions and two ways to practise: Guided Listening with optional clues that do not reveal the answer, and Independent Listening without hints. Suggested answers appear after the activity.

Cada audición tendrá audio real, cuatro o cinco preguntas y dos modalidades. Las pistas orientarán sin dar la respuesta.
