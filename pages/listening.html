Listening Comprehension

Leaving Certificate · Ordinary Level

Listen, understand and practise at your own pace. / Escucha, comprende y practica a tu ritmo.

How to practise / Cómo practicar

Each listening has an audio recording, five questions and two ways to practise: Guided Listening with optional clues that do not reveal the answer, and Independent Listening without hints. Suggested answers appear after the activity.

Cada audición tiene audio real, cinco preguntas y dos modalidades. Las pistas orientan sin dar la respuesta.

Nineteen activities available / Diecinueve actividades disponibles. Both modes use the same questions; Guided offers optional hints. / Ambas modalidades usan las mismas preguntas; Guided ofrece pistas opcionales.
