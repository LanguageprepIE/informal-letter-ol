Study plan / Plan de estudio

6 blocks · 24 flexible study weeks / 6 bloques · 24 semanas flexibles

Your route to June / Tu recorrido hasta junio

Each block has four study weeks, with four main sessions, Monday to Thursday, plus a short weekend review. Start where you need to and pause for holidays, school exams or extra revision. These are study weeks, not fixed calendar dates. / Cada bloque contiene cuatro semanas de trabajo. Puedes hacer pausas para vacaciones, exámenes o repaso.

This route is a selection, not a checklist of everything on the site. Repeating an earlier activity with less help is part of learning. / No tienes que completar todos los recursos; volver a una actividad con menos ayuda también es avanzar.

Choose your weekly pace / Elige tu ritmo

Choose separately in each week. Balanced is recommended to start. Every pace has the same four main tasks; a quiet week is a useful week too.

  • Quiet / Tranquilo: 20 min Monday–Thursday + 10 min at the weekend = 90 min/week.
  • Balanced / Equilibrado: 25 min Monday–Thursday + 15 min at the weekend = 115 min/week.
  • Intensive / Intensivo: 30 min Monday–Thursday + 20 min at the weekend = 140 min/week.

Start with the 20-minute main task. In extra time, correct and recall; you do not need to start another full activity. Weekend review and extra practice have no required ticks. Pause or repeat a week whenever useful. Your task ticks stay the same when you change pace.

How to use your sessions / Cómo aprovechar cada sesión
  • Language: spend about 10 minutes on the main vocabulary group: Understand → Read → Complete/check. Use the English help and correct your answers. Use the remaining 10 minutes for the linked grammar or Sentence Building task. One group is enough; continue next time if needed.
  • Reading: answer first, then check the supporting phrase. Use Guided when needed; do not memorise whole texts. / Responde y busca la evidencia.
  • Listening: read the questions, listen twice and answer; only then use the transcript and highlighted evidence to review. Guided hints are optional. Choose an available speed that helps you follow the audio. / La transcripción es para revisar después.
  • Writing: start with your own attempt, then use models and self-checks. Keep earlier drafts so you can revisit them. The 20 minutes are a study target, not an official exam time limit. / Los 20 minutos orientan el estudio; no son un tiempo oficial de examen.

If a task takes longer, continue next time. If Independent is too difficult, use Guided and try again later. / Si necesitas más tiempo o ayuda, utilízalos.

Extra practice and buffer weeks / Práctica adicional y semanas de margen

Before June, use spare weeks to return to the questions or writing patterns you still find difficult. You can pause a block or spend another week on it; you do not need to catch up by doubling your workload. / Usa las semanas de margen para consolidar, sin duplicar la carga.

Optional alternative listenings, one at a time: School news, Food donations, Youth library, Canary Islands weather or Central Spain weather. These are alternatives or extra practice, not additional weekly requirements.

You can also use a complete official past paper with its recording when your teacher provides the matching materials. Follow that paper’s instructions. This study plan is a learning route, not a complete mock exam.

The focus timer is independent: use its 20-minute focus period for the main task, then continue for 5 or 10 minutes if you chose a longer session.
